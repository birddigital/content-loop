## Posting Best Practices

### LinkedIn
- **Best times**: 8am, 12pm, 5pm EST
- **Length**: 1,500-3,000 characters (long-form performs well)
- **Format**: Use emojis, bullet points, white space
- **Engagement**: Respond to comments within 1 hour
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 relevant hashtags (max 10)
- **Media**: Carousel PDFs, images, documents

### Twitter/X
- **Best times**: 9am, 3pm, 8pm EST
- **Length**: <280 characters (use threads for long content)
- **Format**: 1-3 threads/day max
- **Engagement**: Quote tweets, replies, retweets
- **Hashtags**: 1-3 hashtags (more = less reach)
- **Media**: Images, short videos, GIFs

### YouTube Community
- **Best times**: 10am, 2pm, 6pm EST
- **Length**: 500-1,000 characters
- **Format**: Questions, behind-the-scenes, updates
- **Engagement**: Pin important posts
- **Hashtags**: 5-10 hashtags
- **Media**: Thumbnails, shorts, community images

### Threads
- **Best times**: 7am, 12pm, 7pm EST
- **Length**: <500 characters
- **Format**: Casual, POV, questions
- **Engagement**: Reply to comments
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 hashtags
- **Media**: Carousel posts (5 slides max)
